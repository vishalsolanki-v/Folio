# Folio privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Folio is a file viewer and editor that works entirely on your phone.

**Folio collects nothing and has no internet permission.** Folio does not upload your files, Recents or settings.
There is no account, analytics, advertising or crash-reporting service.

**Files stay on your phone while Folio handles them.** To keep files from apps like WhatsApp or email in Recents
after the sending app takes them away, Folio can keep a private copy inside its own storage (up to 300 MB; you can
turn this off in Settings). Your list of recent files, pinned files and settings are stored only on your phone.

If you choose **Share** or **Open with another app**, Folio gives the app you select access to
the file. That app may copy or send it elsewhere under its own privacy practices. Folio does not control what
another app does with a file you chose to hand to it.

**You are in control.** Settings → "Clear recents and copies" deletes everything Folio keeps. Uninstalling Folio
deletes all of it too.

**Permissions.** Folio asks for no permissions. It can only open files you choose: through Android's file picker,
folders you add, or apps that send a file to Folio.

**Children.** Folio doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
