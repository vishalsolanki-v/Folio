# Folio privacy policy

_Last updated: 28 October 2026_

Folio is a file viewer and editor that works entirely on your phone.

**Folio collects nothing.** It has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. There is no
account, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting service.

**Your files stay on your phone.** Folio reads the files you open in it. To keep files from apps like WhatsApp or
email in your Recents after the sharing app takes them away, Folio can keep a private copy inside its own storage
(up to 300 MB; you can turn this off in Settings). Your list of recent files, pinned files and settings are stored
only on your phone.

**You are in control.** Settings → "Clear recents and copies" deletes everything Folio keeps. Uninstalling Folio
deletes all of it too.

**Permissions.** Folio asks for no permissions. It can only open files you choose: through Android's file picker,
folders you add, or apps that send a file to Folio.

**Children.** Folio doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.
